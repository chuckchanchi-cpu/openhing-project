## 第一組：動物與環境（Noise / Animals 篇）
### habitat
- 解釋：動植物棲息、生活的環境
- 例句：Every habitat on Earth is affected by human noise.
### predator
- 解釋：捕食其他動物的動物；捕食者
- 例句：Some predators benefit from the noise because it drives prey out of hiding.
### prey
- 解釋：被捕食的動物；獵物
- 例句：The noise flushed the prey out of hiding.
### disrupt
- 解釋：擾亂、打斷
- 例句：Ship engines disrupt the communication of whales and dolphins.
### disorientate
- 解釋：使迷失方向
- 例句：Noise can disorientate marine animals.
### population
- 解釋：種群數量；人口
- 例句：When animals leave, their population sizes fall.

## 第二組：健康與科學（Fungus / Autism 篇）
### fungus
- 解釋：真菌（複數 fungi）
- 例句：Candida auris is a dangerous fungus.
### yeast
- 解釋：酵母菌；單細胞真菌
- 例句：C. auris is a yeast, not a mushroom.
### antifungal
- 解釋：抗真菌的
- 例句：The new strain resists every known antifungal drug.
### resistant
- 解釋：有抵抗力的；抗藥的
- 例句：The bacteria are resistant to antibiotics.
### infection
- 解釋：感染
- 例句：Her infection did not go away after 13 medicines.
### persistent
- 解釋：持續不退的；頑固的
- 例句：The infection persisted even after treatment.
### vulnerable
- 解釋：易受傷害的；脆弱的
- 例句：Intensive care patients are particularly vulnerable.
### contagious
- 解釋：會傳染的
- 例句：Being drug-resistant does not mean the fungus is more contagious.
### shield
- 解釋：盾牌；保護物
- 例句：Intact skin is a strong shield against germs.
### gene
- 解釋：基因
- 例句：ZFX is a gene on the X chromosome.
### chromosome
- 解釋：染色體
- 例句：Girls have two X chromosomes.

## 第三組：日常與規則（Disney / Charlie 篇）
### accommodate
- 解釋：容納
- 例句：Each room can accommodate up to four guests.
### maximum
- 解釋：最大量；最多
- 例句：The maximum number of guests is four.
### prohibited
- 解釋：被禁止的
- 例句：Bird feeding is prohibited.
### permitted
- 解釋：被允許的
- 例句：Riding bicycles is not permitted inside the hotel.
### exception
- 解釋：例外
- 例句：Service animals are an exception to the rule.
### treat
- 解釋：款待；美食
- 例句：Chocolate is Charlie's favourite treat.
### grandparents
- 解釋：祖父母
- 例句：Charlie lives with his four grandparents.
### persistent
- 解釋：堅持不懈的（另一義）
- 例句：Be persistent and never give up.
