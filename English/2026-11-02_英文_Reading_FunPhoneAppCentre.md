## 📖 Reading Passage: Fun Phone App Centre

Gillian is reading a leaflet introducing some phone apps. The leaflet promotes **four phone apps** available from an apps centre. From 1st to 31st May, you can get four codes by downloading all four phone apps.

### App 1: Let's Chat!
> Want to find your friends anywhere, anytime? Use our app to talk to them through instant messages, calls and video calls. You can also make new friends in different countries by choosing from the "Find Friends" button. If you get bored, check out "Puzzle games" with your friends in the app. There are **five new puzzles every day**. You can also play puzzle games with your friends and chat with them at the same time!

### App 2: All Around the World
> Travel to places all around the world with just one click on your phone. Choose a country and watch a video about the place. You can also book cheap flights and hotels using our app.

### App 3: Friendly Animal Centre
> Help Elsa and Hannah run their animal centre and take care of all the lovely animals! There are **200 cartoon pets** for you to choose from, including dogs, cats, hamsters, etc. You can do lots of things with them, such as bathing, feeding and exercising. You can even visit your friends' centres and play with their pets!

### App 4: Who Are You?
> Have you ever wondered how you would look if you were the opposite gender or 100 years old? Take a selfie on the app to find out! You can also add stickers on photos. Just try to add a funny bat sticker on your head!

## ✍️ Questions (1-6) — Multiple Choice

### 1. From the leaflet, what do we know about the promotion?
- A. The promotion lasts for three months.
- B. Each app requires a code to download.
- C. People must download the apps on a website.
- **D. Let's Chat! is a free app.** ✅

### 2. Which of the following is TRUE about Let's Chat!?
- A. It only works for people who live in the same country.
- B. It only works for people who use video calls.
- C. People can look for new friends in different countries. ✅
- D. People can chat with friends about puzzles only.

### 3. In the description of Friendly Animal Centre, what does "them" refer to?
- A. the 200 cartoon pets ✅
- B. Elsa and Hannah
- C. the user's friends
- D. the pets in a friend's pet shop

### 4. Which of the following is NOT true about All Around the World?
- A. People can find out information about different countries on their phones.
- B. People can buy food and clothes from different places using the app. ✅（NOT true）
- C. People can book flights and hotels using the app.
- D. Short videos about different countries are provided.

### 5. For Who Are You?, how many functions are there?
- A. 1
- B. 3
- C. 4 ✅
- D. 5
（Functions: opposite gender selfie, 100 years old selfie, add stickers, add bat sticker = 4）

### 6. Study the following chart and decide which might be the two most popular apps in Fun Phone Apps Centre among young people. Write the letter (A-D) in each blank.
- (i) The most popular app: **A. Let's Chat!** ✅
- (ii) The second most popular app: **C. All Around the World** ✅
