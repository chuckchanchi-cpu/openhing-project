## 📖 文法重點：祈使句嘅間接引語

| 直接引語 | 間接引語結構 | 例子 |
|----------|--------------|------|
| 祈使句（命令） | **tell + 人 + to + 原形動詞** | "Take it." → He told me **to take** it. |
| 禮貌請求（Please…） | **ask + 人 + to + 原形動詞** | "Please stay." → They asked me **to stay**. |
| 否定命令（Don't…） | **tell/ask + 人 + not to + 原形動詞** | "Don't run." → She told me **not to run**. |
| 代詞轉換 | 要轉返說話者角度 | "try **my** apple" → to try **her** apple；"marry **me**" → to marry **him** |

⚠️ 三大易錯位：
1. to 後面永遠用**原形動詞**（to take ✅ / to took ❌）
2. 否定 = **not to**（唔係 don't to / to not）
3. 代詞要轉（my→her、me→him、us→them、I→he/she）

## 📝 題目與正確答案

### 1. the Queen → the Huntsman："Take Snow White into the forest."（tell）
**The Queen told the Huntsman to take Snow White into the forest.** ✅（印刷例句）
- 💡 命令句 → tell + 人 + to + 原形。

### 2. the Huntsman → Snow White："Run away!"（tell）
**The Huntsman told Snow White to run away.**
- 💡 祈使句直接加 to + 原形（run）。

### 3. the Seven Dwarfs → Snow White："Please stay with us."（ask）
**The Seven Dwarfs asked Snow White to stay with them.**
- 💡 Please… 禮貌請求 → **ask**；⚠️ 代詞轉換：**us → them**（小矮人角度）。

### 4. the Seven Dwarfs → Snow White："Don't let anyone in."（tell）
**The Seven Dwarfs told Snow White not to let anyone in.**
- 💡 否定命令 → **not to** + 原形；⚠️ 唔好寫 don't let。

### 5. the Witch → Snow White："Please try my delicious apple."（ask）
**The Witch asked Snow White to try her delicious apple.**
- 💡 Please… → ask；⚠️ 代詞轉換：**my → her**（巫婆角度）。

### 6. the Prince → Snow White："Please marry me."（ask）
**The Prince asked Snow White to marry him.**
- 💡 Please… → ask；⚠️ 代詞轉換：**me → him**（王子角度）。
