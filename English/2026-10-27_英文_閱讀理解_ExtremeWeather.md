## 📖 A. Reading Skill: Analyzing（True / False）

- **Analyzing** = 高階閱讀技巧：唔係淨係知「發生咩事」（Comprehension），要當「reading detective」搵出作者**點寫、點解咁寫**
- **True（✓）**：句子同原文**意思完全一致**（就算用唔同較易嘅字）
- **False（✗）**：改咗事實、調亂數字、用 tricky words（always / never / all / no one）令句子錯誤

**三步搵答案：**
1. **Underline Key Words**：圈出人名、日期、數字、動作詞
2. **Scan the Text**：快速掃文搵關鍵詞／同義概念
3. **Compare Closely**：題目句同原文句逐字對照，檢查意思係咪完全一致

**⚠️ Watch Out for Traps！** 唔好見到一個熟悉字眼就揀 True — 陷阱題會用原文嘅字但**完全改咗意思**，要讀完整句先答！

## 📖 B. 報道文重點：Extreme weather events around the world

| 段落 | 內容 |
|------|------|
| 香港 | 今年多次極端天氣：天文台**兩次**懸掛 **10 號風球**（颱風 **Wipha 韋帕**、**Ragasa 樺加沙**）；上次一年內兩個 10 號係 **1964 年** |
| 圖說 | Ragasa 令香港嚴重水浸，尤其水浸黑點如**杏花邨（Heng Fa Chuen）** |
| 香港雨季 | 極端降水頻繁：**紅雨 16 次、黑雨 5 次**，破紀錄 |
| 全球 | 歐洲和非洲受**熱浪、乾旱、水災**影響；美國加州、南韓、加拿大 2025 年有**山火**；氣候災害令生命財產損失、生產力下降 |
| 原因 | **全球暖化**：平均氣溫上升 → 大氣變暖 → 更強風暴、更強熱浪、更大雨 |
| 行動 | 採納環保做法（adopting environmental practices），令世界更可持續 |

## 📝 C. 題目與正確答案

**1. What is NOT TRUE about extreme weather in Europe and Africa this summer?**
- A. Europe has experienced severe heatwaves.（✓ 原文）
- B. Many African countries have faced prolonged drought conditions.（✓ 原文 droughts）
- **C. Flooding has been rare and insignificant in both Europe and Africa. ✅（答案：NOT TRUE）**
- D. People experience heatwaves, droughts and floods.（✓ 原文）
- 💡 (ii) 證據：「countries in Europe and Africa have been adversely affected by heatwaves, droughts, **and floods** this summer」— 水災都有，C 話「罕見且微不足道」係錯。

**2. What is TRUE about Typhoon Ragasa's effect on Hong Kong?**
- A. It caused droughts.｜**B. It caused severe floods. ✅**｜C. It brought only light rain.｜D. It had no impact on Hong Kong.
- 💡 (ii) 證據：圖說「**Ragasa caused severe floods in Hong Kong**, especially in the flood-prone areas such as Heng Fa Chuen」。

**3. Why are extreme weather events becoming more common around the world?**
- **A. Global warming makes the Earth warmer. ✅**｜B. There is less rainfall worldwide.｜C. The Earth is getting cooler.｜D. More forests are growing everywhere.
- 💡 (ii) 證據：「**global warming** has been one of the reasons… the Earth's atmosphere becomes warmer, potentially leading to stronger storms, more intense heatwaves, and heavier rainfall」。

**4. Which is NOT mentioned as an extreme weather event experienced this year?**
- A. Typhoons in Hong Kong（✓）｜B. Heatwaves in Europe（✓）｜**C. Earthquakes in South Korea ✅（答案：NOT mentioned）**｜D. Wildfires in Canada（✓）
- 💡 (ii) 證據：南韓係「**wildfires**」— 原文冇提地震。⚠️ 逆向題陷阱：南韓出現過，但事件係山火唔係地震。

**5. What changes could people make to reduce extreme weather events?**（短答）
- 學生答：**We can live greener.** ✅
- 💡 參考答案：adopt environmental practices / live more sustainably（例：少用膠袋、節能、乘搭公共交通）。

## 🎯 學習重點
1. True/False 三步：圈關鍵詞 → 掃原文 → 逐字對照
2. NOT 題逆向思維：逐項核對原文，「事件＋地點」要**雙重啱**先算（南韓=山火，唔係地震）
3. 證據題 (ii) 要引原文句子/詞組
4. ⚠️ 陷阱：原文字眼＋改動意思（「floods」出現過但 C 話「rare and insignificant」）
