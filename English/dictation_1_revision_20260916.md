## B. II. What will the children do for charities?（孩子們會為慈善機構做什麼？）— 20分

**要求：** 看圖片，用 **'so'** 寫出完整的句子

### 1. Karen: homeless / help prepare meals
> **I want to help the homeless, so I will help prepare meals for them.**

### 2. Ben: hiking and helping others / go on a charity walk
> **I like hiking and helping others, so I will go on a charity walk in a country park.**

### 3. Sue: blind people / sell flags
> **I want to collect money to help blind people, so I will sell flags in the street.**

### 4. Kate: old people / visit
> **I want to help old people, so I will visit an elderly home.**

### 5. Jack: animals / adopt
> **I want to help animals, so I will adopt a pet from the SPCA.**

---

### 💡 'so' 句型結構：
**[原因/想法] + , + so + [結果/行動]**

| 前半句（原因/想法） | so | 後半句（結果/行動） |
|---------------------|----|---------------------|
| I want to help the homeless | so | I will help prepare meals for them. |
| I like hiking and helping others | so | I will go on a charity walk in a country park. |
| I want to collect money to help blind people | so | I will sell flags in the street. |
| I want to help old people | so | I will visit an elderly home. |
| I want to help animals | so | I will adopt a pet from the SPCA. |

## 💡 考試技巧

### 聽寫技巧：
1. 第一次聽：快速瀏覽全文，了解大意
2. 第二次聽：寫下關鍵詞和句子
3. 第三次聽：檢查拼寫和標點

### 'so' 句型技巧：
1. 先看圖片提示
2. 找出「想要什麼」或「喜歡什麼」（前半句）
3. 再写出「會做什麼」（後半句）
4. 中間加 ', so' 連接

### 時間管理：
- 聽寫部分：每段約5-7分鐘
- 句型部分：每題約2分鐘
- 總時間控制在不超過課文長度
