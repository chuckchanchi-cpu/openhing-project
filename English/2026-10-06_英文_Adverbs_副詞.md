## 📖 知識點：Adverbs 副詞

### What is an Adverb?
An adverb describes **how** an action is done. It modifies a **verb**.

> We use adverbs to describe verbs.

## 📝 Part 2: Frequency Adverbs 頻率副詞

### Frequency Scale:

| Adverb | Frequency | 中文 |
|--------|-----------|------|
| **always** | 100% | 總是 |
| **usually** | ~80% | 通常 |
| **often** | ~60% | 經常 |
| **sometimes** | ~40% | 有時 |
| **seldom** | ~10% | 很少 |
| **never** | 0% | 從不 |

### Word Order for Frequency Adverbs:
**Subject + adverb + verb + object**

Examples:
- I **always** brush my teeth.
- She **usually** walks to school.
- He **sometimes** plays football.
- They **never** eat junk food.

## 🎯 Grammar Rules

### Adverb Placement:
1. **Manner adverbs** (how): usually after the verb
   - She sings **beautifully**.
2. **Frequency adverbs** (how often): before the main verb
   - I **always** eat breakfast.
3. **Time adverbs** (when): usually at the end
   - He wakes up **early**.

### Common Mistakes:
❌ She sings **beautiful**. (adjective, not adverb)
✅ She sings **beautifully**. (adverb)

❌ He runs **quickly fast**.
✅ He runs **fast**. (fast is both adjective and adverb)
