# 2026-10-06 英文 - Look and Write (看圖寫作)

## 課堂內容
**日期：** 2026-10-06
**類型：** 看圖寫作 (Look and Write)

## 練習要求
- 看圖寫出完整句子
- 每句不少於 **5 個字** (at least 5 words)

## 圖片描述
圖一：課室場景，有學生坐喺枱前面，老師企喺前面教書
圖二：示範句子

## 參考句子
1. This is a bear.
2. These are birds.
3. I am a boy.

## 學生示範
- **Name:** Hayden
- **Age:** 8
- **Likes:** pizza
- **句子:** My name is Hayden. I am 8. I like pizza.

## 重點詞彙
- This is (這是 - 單數)
- These are (呢啲 - 複數)
- I am (我是)
- I like (我鍾意)

## 句型結構
| 句型 | 例子 |
|------|------|
| This is + 單數名詞 | This is a bear. |
| These are + 複數名詞 | These are birds. |
| I am + 描述 | I am a boy. |
| I like + 名詞 | I like pizza. |

## 練習題目
**Look at the picture. Write sentences about it.**

Write at least 3 sentences about the classroom picture. Use the sentence patterns you learned:
- This is / These are...
- I am / We are...
- I like / We like...

**Requirements:**
- Each sentence must have at least 5 words
- Use correct grammar and spelling
- Be creative and describe what you see in the picture
