## 📖 一、句子結構 Subject–Verb–Object
- 句子開頭用**大階字母**，結尾用**句號／問號／驚嘆號**
- 簡單句結構：**Subject + Verb + Object**；額外資料（extra information）通常放**句尾**
- 例：Paul visited an elderly home last Sunday.｜Mary is making a chocolate cake in the kitchen.｜Simon donated one million dollars to the charity.

## 📖 二、11 個疑問詞（Question words）
| 疑問詞 | 問咩 | 例子（答案） |
|--------|------|-------------|
| **What** | 東西名 | What did he buy?（a cake） |
| **When** | 時間 | When did he go to sleep?（ten o'clock） |
| **Where** | 地點 | Where is he going?（school） |
| **Which** | 一組入面揀一個 | Which book do you like?（the book with a green cover） |
| **Who** | 做動作嘅人 | Who is Mr Chow?（our principal） |
| **Whose** | 擁有者 | Whose pencil is this?（May's） |
| **Why** | 原因 | Why couldn't he go?（he was sick） |
| **How** | 方式 | How do you go to school?（by train） |
| **How many** | 數目 | How many books?（six books） |
| **How far** | 距離 | How far?（one kilometre） |
| **How long** | 時間長度 | How long does it take?（15 minutes） |

## 📝 四、Exercise 2：組句（學生習作）
1. mum / is stirring / the soup → **Mum is stirring the soup.** ✅
2. on a walkathon / John / goes → **John goes on a walkathon.** ✅（漏句號）
3. we / takes care of old people / can / which / a centre / visit → **We can visit a centre which takes care of old people.** ⚠️（學生次序錯＋ takes 錯位）

## 📝 五、Exercise 3：完整句答題（標準答案＋學生常見錯誤）
| # | 問題（提示） | 標準答案 | 學生常見錯 |
|---|------------|---------|-----------|
| 1 | What can we do to help the victims?（donate money） | We can donate money to help the victims. ✅ | |
| 2 | What does the word 'they' refer to?（the flood victims） | The word 'they' **refers** to the flood victims. | refer 漏 s ⚠️ |
| 3 | When did you first come to Hong Kong?（in 1993） | I first **came** to Hong Kong in 1993. | come 未轉過去式 ⚠️ |
| 4 | Where is the information counter?（behind the souvenir shop） | **The information counter is** behind the souvenir shop. | 詞序亂（information is counter ❌）⚠️ |
| 5 | Who does the charity help?（poor people） | The charity **helps** poor people. | help 漏 s ⚠️ |
| 6 | Which kind of ice cream do you like?（chocolate） | I like chocolate ice cream. ✅ | |
| 7 | Why does Tom do voluntary work for the SPCA?（want to help animals） | Tom does voluntary work for the SPCA **because he wants to help animals**. | 用「because」答 Why ⚠️ |
| 8 | How does Oxfam help people?（give food） | Oxfam **helps** people by giving them food. | give 漏 s ⚠️ |
| 9 | How often do you visit Taiwan?（once a year） | I visit Taiwan once a year. ✅ | |
| 10 | How far is it from our home to the post office?（200 metres） | It is 200 metres from our home to the post office. ✅ | |
| 11 | How long does it take to walk to the gift shop?（15 minutes） | It **takes** 15 minutes to walk from our home to the gift shop. ✅ | |

## 🎯 學習重點
1. **完整句 = 主語 + 動詞（+ 受詞）**＋句末標點；片段/從屬子句（Although/Because 開頭）唔算完整句
2. **疑問詞對應**：What=物、When=時、Where=地、Which=揀選、Who=人、Whose=擁有、Why=因、How=式、How many=數、How far=距、How long=時長
3. **答題三檢查**：①完整句 ②主謂一致（he/she/it + s）③時態一致（did → 過去式）
4. ⚠️ 學生常見錯誤（已歸納）：三單漏 s（refers/helps）、過去式漏轉（came）、詞序亂、Why 要用 because 答
