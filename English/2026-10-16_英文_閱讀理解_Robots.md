## 📝 題目與正確答案

### Q1. Why are robots popular with students in schools?
- A. They look like cartoon characters.
- B. They can talk to students.
- **C. They are fun and can help with their learning. ✅**
- D. They can teach students computer languages.
- 💡 分析：第二段「playful and child-friendly qualities」+「help students in STEM classes」→ 有趣 + 幫學習。A 係玩具層面，唔係學校受歡迎嘅原因。

### Q2. Which function of robots is NOT mentioned in the article?
- A. Join metals together ✅（第四段 joining hot metals together）
- B. Talk to people ✅（第五段 hold simple conversations）
- **C. Take photos of customers ✅（答案：文中只有顧客自己同機械人 selfies，冇話機械人幫顧客影相）**
- D. Write articles ✅（第五段 robot produce an article）
- 💡 分析：**逆向題**——三個有提及、一個冇。做題技巧：逐項喺原文劃線核對，剩低嗰個就係答案。
- ⚠️ 陷阱：「take selfies with the robots」係**顧客自己影**，唔等於機械人「take photos of customers」！

### Q3. From the last paragraph, what does the writer think about robots?
- A. The writer believes robots will soon take over all teaching jobs.
- B. The writer thinks robots are dangerous and should not be used in the classrooms.
- **C. The writer thinks robots have some limits and cannot fully replace human teachers. ✅**
- D. The writer believes robots can only help teachers with easy tasks.
- 💡 分析：末段「But will robots completely replace teachers? **Not likely.**」+「This is still a technical challenge」→ 有局限、唔會完全取代。D 過度簡化（仲有其他應用）。

### Q4. What might customers enjoy about the experience at the ice cream shop with robots?
- A. Robots are more efficient than human workers.
- **B. Customers can take selfies with the robots, enhancing their experience. ✅**
- C. Robots do not make mistakes when serving ice cream.
- D. The robots are programmed to tell jokes when serving.
- 💡 分析：第三段「customers can also take selfies with the robots, which makes their experience even more special」直接對應 B。

### Q5. What challenge do teachers face that robots cannot currently address?
- A. Preparing lesson plans.
- B. Teaching vocabulary and numbers.
- **C. Responding to each student's immediate needs and providing support. ✅**
- D. Carrying exercise books.
- 💡 分析：末段「teachers also need to pay attention to every student's immediate response and provide timely support. This is still a technical challenge」→ C。B/D 都係機械人做得到嘅嘢。

### Q6. ONE-word 填詞（Kate's blog entry）
Robots have a variety of uses. At school, they can help students (i) **learn** in STEM classes. In (ii) **hospitals**, they can help in heart surgery. They can do heavy jobs in factories. They can even ...
- (i) learn ✅（help students learn in STEM classes）
- (ii) hospitals ✅（平行結構：At school → In hospitals → in factories，全部係地點）
- 💡 分析：**ONE word** 限定 + 要合文法。(ii) 答「addition」（In addition）文法都通，但同後句「in factories」嘅地點平行結構對唔上，**hospitals** 最貼。
- ⚠️ 陷阱：學生先寫「supporting」（畫咗 ✗）——唔合文法（In supporting ❌）；要諗介詞 in 後面嘅名詞。
