# Unit 3 - Environmental Protection 環境保護
## Adverbs of Manner 方式副詞

### 1. 常見方式副詞

| 副詞 | 中文意思 | 例句 |
|------|----------|------|
| carefully | 小心地 | Please read the passage carefully. 請仔細閱讀文章。 |
| slowly | 慢地 | The turtle walks slowly. 烏龜走得慢。 |
| quietly | 安靜地 | The students sit quietly in the library. 學生安靜地坐在圖書館。 |
| happily | 快樂地 | The children are playing happily in the park. 孩子在公園快樂地玩耍。 |
| loudly | 大聲地 | Do not speak loudly in the hospital. 不要在医院大聲說話。 |
| fast | 快地 | The rabbit runs very fast. 兔子跑得非常快。 |
| well | 好地 | She sings very well. 她歌唱得非常好。 |

### 2. 句型：Verb + Object + Adverb

**結構：** 動詞 + 賓語 + 方式副詞

| 例子 | 解釋 |
|------|------|
| Please **listen to the teacher carefully**. | listen (V) + teacher (O) + carefully (Adv) |
| We should **use the bags again**. | use (V) + bags (O) + again (Adv) |
| The boy **helps his mother happily**. | helps (V) + mother (O) + happily (Adv) |
| They **keep the classroom clean**. | keep (V) + classroom (O) + clean (Adj) |

### 3. 練習題

#### 選擇正確的方式副詞

1. The students should listen to the teacher ______.
   - A. careful
   - B. carefully
   - C. care
   - **答案：** B. carefully

2. Please ______ turn off the lights when you leave the room.
   - A. careful
   - B. carefully
   - C. care
   - **答案：** B. carefully

3. The children played ______ in the playground.
   - A. happy
   - B. happily
   - C. happiness
   - **答案：** B. happily

4. We must throw the rubbish ______.
   - A. proper
   - B. properly
   - C. proper
   - **答案：** B. properly

5. She speaks English very ______.
   - A. good
   - B. well
   - C. goodly
   - **答案：** B. well

#### 改寫句子

1. The boy reads the book. (slowly)
   → The boy reads the book **slowly**.

2. The girl sings a song. (beautifully)
   → The girl sings a song **beautifully**.

3. The teacher explains the question. (clearly)
   → The teacher explains the question **clearly**.

### 5. 環境保護標語

- **Save the Earth, protect the environment.** 保護地球，愛護環境。
- **Reduce, reuse, recycle.** 減少、重用、回收。
- **Do not throw rubbish everywhere.** 請勿隨地亂扔垃圾。
- **Save water and electricity.** 節約用水用電。
- **Use less plastic.** 減少使用塑膠。

## 語法要點總結

1. **方式副詞的位置：** 修飾動詞， usually放在動詞後面
   - 例：She sings **well**. (好地唱歌)

2. **Verb + Object + Adverb 句型：** 副詞修飾動詞，放在賓語後面
   - 例：Please listen to the teacher **carefully**. (請仔細聽老師說話)

3. **副詞 vs 形容詞：**
   - 形容詞修飾名詞：He is a **careful** student.
   - 副詞修飾動詞：He works **carefully**.
