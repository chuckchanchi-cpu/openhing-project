# 英文語法練習卷 — Unit 1: A Helping Hand（擴寫句子）

## 📋 Sentence Expansion Practice

### 重點技巧：5W1H 擴寫法
用以下問題幫助擴寫句子：
- **Who/What**（主詞）
- **What (do)**（動詞/動作）
- **When**（時間）
- **Where**（地點）
- **How**（方式/感受）
- **Why**（原因）

---

### 範例分析

#### Simple sentence: Eva and I sold flags.

| 擴寫要素 | 內容 |
|----------|------|
| a) Who/What (subject) | Eva and I |
| b) What (do) | sold flags |
| 1. When? | Today |
| 2. Where? | Yuen Long |
| 3. How? | excitedly, with big smiles on our faces |
| 4. Why? | first time doing voluntary work |

**Expanded Sentences:**  
Eva and I sold flags excitedly in Yuen Long today with big smiles on our faces because it was our first time doing voluntary work.

---

### 練習題解析

#### Exercise 1: We were selling flags.

| 擴寫要素 | 答案 |
|----------|------|
| a) Who/What (subject) | We |
| b) What (do) | were selling flags |
| 1. When? | Yesterday |
| 2. Where? | Yuen Long |
| 3. Who? | Residents |
| 4. How? (feelings) | Excited |
| 5. Why? | I could raise money to help people in need |

**Expanded Sentences:**  
Yesterday, we were selling flags in Yuen Long to the residents. I felt excited because I could raise money to help people in need.

---

#### Exercise 2: Many passersby came to us.

| 擴寫方式 | 答案 |
|----------|------|
| Add adjective | Many **kind** passersby came to us. |
| Add adjective | Many **kind-hearted** and **generous** passersby came to us. |

---

#### Exercise 3: A man snatched my money collection bag.

| 擴寫方式 | 答案 |
|----------|------|
| Add adjective | A **quick** man in a **mask** snatched my money collection bag. |
| Add adjective phrase | A **young, quick** man in a **mask** snatched my money collection bag. |
| Add adjective phrase | A **young, quick** man in a **mask** and **cap** snatched my money collection bag. |

## 📝 關鍵單字與短語

### 动词短语
- sell flags（賣旗）
- come to us（來到我們身邊）
- snatch my money collection bag（搶走我的捐款袋）

### 形容詞
- kind（善良的）
- kind-hearted（樂於助人的）
- generous（慷慨的）
- quick（快速的）
- young（年輕的）
- mask（口罩/面罩）
- cap（帽子）

### 副詞與感受
- excitedly（興奮地）
- with big smiles on our faces（帶著燦爛的笑容）
- excited（感到興奮）
