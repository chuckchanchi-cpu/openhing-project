## 一、Writing Skills: Sentence Expansion Practice（句子擴寫練習）

### ✅ 範例分析

| Simple sentence | Eva and I sold flags. |
|-----------------|----------------------|
| a) Who/What (subject) | Eva and I |
| b) What (do) | sold flags |

**5W1H 擴寫提示：**
| 問題 | 答案 |
|------|------|
| 1. When? | Today |
| 2. Where? | Yuen Long |
| 3. How? | excitedly, with big smiles on our faces |
| 4. Why? | first time doing voluntary work |

**Expanded Sentences:**
> Eva and I sold flags excitedly in Yuen Long today with big smiles on our faces because it was our first time doing voluntary work.

---

### 📝 Exercise 1: We were selling flags.

**Simple sentence:** We were selling flags.

**5W1H 擴寫：**
| 問題 | 答案 |
|------|------|
| a) Who/What (subject) | We |
| b) What (do) | were selling flags |
| 1. When? | Yesterday |
| 2. Where? | Yuen Long Plaza |
| 3. Who? | the residents / passersby |
| 4. How? (feelings) | excited |
| 5. Why? | I could raise money to help people in need |

**Expanded Sentences:**
> Yesterday, we were selling flags near Yuen Long Plaza to the passersby. I felt excited because I could raise money to help people in need.

---

### 📝 Exercise 2: Many passersby came to us.

**Simple sentence:** Many passersby came to us.

**加形容詞練習：**
| 擴寫方式 | 答案 |
|----------|------|
| Add adjective (1個) | Many **kind** passersby came to us. |
| Add adjective (2個 + and) | Many **kind-hearted** and **generous** passersby came to us. |

---

### 📝 Exercise 3: A man snatched my money collection bag.

**Simple sentence:** A man snatched my money collection bag.

**逐步加形容詞短语：**
| 擴寫方式 | 答案 |
|----------|------|
| Add adjective | A **quick/tall** man in a **black T-shirt** snatched... |
| Add adjective phrase | A **young/tall** man in a **mask/spotted shirt** snatched... |
| Add adjective phrase (更完整) | A **young/tall** man in a **black T-shirt/spotted shirt** and **wearing a cap** snatched... |

## 二、Writing Tasks: Picture Description（圖片描述）

### 🖼️ Picture 1: Flag-selling Activity（賣旗活動）

**Vocabulary words for the picture:**
- gather / volunteer
- separate everyone into groups of two

**Tips & Questions:**
- What time was it? → At 7:30 am
- Where were you and Eva? → at a flag-selling activity
- What were you doing? → gathering volunteers, separating into groups
- How did you feel? Why? → excited, happy

**Sample Answer (15-20 words):**
> At 7:30 am, Eva and I were volunteers at a flag-selling activity. We gathered all people and separated everyone into groups of two. We felt happy because we could help others.

---

- What were you doing?
- What happened?
- How did you feel? Why?

## 💡 寫作技巧總結

### 技巧1: 5W1H 擴寫法
**用於：** 把簡單句擴寫成完整句子

**步驟：**
1. 找出主詞 (Who/What)
2. 找出動詞 (What do)
3. 用以下問題補充細節：
   - When? (時間)
   - Where? (地點)
   - Who? (對象)
   - How? (方式/感受)
   - Why? (原因)

**範例：**
> Simple: We were selling flags.  
> Expanded: Yesterday, we were selling flags near Yuen Long Plaza to the passersby. I felt excited because I could raise money to help people in need.

---

### 技巧2: 加形容詞 (Add Adjective)
**用於：** 讓描述更詳細

**規則：**
- 1個形容詞：Many **kind** passersby
- 2個形容詞（and連接）：Many **kind-hearted** and **generous** passersby

**位置：** 放在名詞前面

---

### 技巧3: 加形容詞短语 (Add Adjective Phrase)
**用於：** 更詳細地描述人物或事物

**結構：**
- 形容詞 + 介系詞短语
- 例：A **young** man in a **mask** and **wearing a cap**

**層次：**
1. 基礎：A quick man in a black T-shirt
2. 進階：A young man in a mask
3. 完整：A young man in a black T-shirt and wearing a cap

---
