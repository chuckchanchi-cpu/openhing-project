# 英文語法練習卷 — Unit 1: A Helping Hand

## 📋 Grammar 1: so（原因和結果）

### 重點規則
- **so** 連接原因和結果
- 前面加 **逗號 (,)**
- 結構：`原因 + , + so + 結果`

### 例句分析
| 題號 | 原因 | so | 結果 |
|------|------|----|------|
| 1 | Ella wants to raise money for a school in Africa | so | she will go on a walkathon next weekend |
| 2 | Maisy loves talking to old people | so | she often visits an elderly home |
| 3 | Sam enjoys cooking | so | he helps make meals for the homeless |
| 4 | Billy helps organize a fund-raising fair | so | he is asking shops to donate things to sell |
| 5 | Kay is good at taking care of others | so | he does voluntary work at a center for children with special needs |

---

## 📋 Grammar 2: who / which（關係代詞）

### 重點規則
| 代詞 | 用法 | 例子 |
|------|------|------|
| **who** | 指人 (people) | A volunteer **who** helps others |
| **which** | 指物/動物 (things/animals) | The SPCA is a charity **which** saves animals |

### 例句分析

#### Class 6A
1. It is a food charity **which** brings meals to old people.
2. They are going to have a plant sale. It will take place on Friday after school.

#### Class 6B
3. They are collecting old toys for children **who** are poor. The children do not have money to buy toys.
4. They are also collecting clothes **which** are thick. The clothes can help the homeless keep warm in winter.

#### Class 6C
5. They will do voluntary work at a children's hospital **which** is near our school.
6. They are going to play games with sick children **who** need to stay in hospital for a long time.

---

## 💡 解題技巧

### Grammar 1: so 的運用
1. **先找原因**：什麼事情發生？（想要籌錢、喜歡聊天、擅長煮餸...）
2. **再找結果**：因為這個原因，做了什麼行動？（參加行山、去探訪老人院...）
3. **加上 so**：用逗號分隔，連接兩個句子

### Grammar 2: who vs which
1. **看被修飾的名詞**：
   - 是人 → 用 **who**
   - 是物/動物 → 用 **which**
2. **常見搭配**：
   - person/volunteer/children/patient → **who**
   - charity/hospital/clothes/toys → **which**

## 📝 練習重點

### Grammar 1 關鍵詞彙
- raise money for...（為...籌款）
- go on a walkathon（參加行山活動）
- visit an elderly home（探訪老人院）
- help make meals for the homeless（為無家可歸者準備食物）
- organize a fund-raising fair（組織籌款集市）
- ask shops to donate（請商店捐贈）
- do voluntary work（做義工）
- take care of others（照顧他人）

### Grammar 2 常見名詞
- food charity（食物慈善機構）
- plant sale（植物拍賣）
- old toys（舊玩具）
- thick clothes（厚衣服）
- voluntary work（義工服務）
- children's hospital（兒童醫院）
- sick children（生病兒童）
