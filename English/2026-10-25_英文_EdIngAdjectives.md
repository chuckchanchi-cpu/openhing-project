# 英文 — -ed and -ing Adjectives（P.19-20）

> 來源：Justan Edu Discord #general（2026-10-25 上載工作紙，日期 23rd Oct 2026）｜記錄：2026-10-25｜適用：小六
> 主題：-ed 形容詞（描述感受）vs -ing 形容詞（描述令人有感受嘅事物）

## 📖 文法重點

| | 用法 | 例子 |
|---|------|------|
| **-ed adjectives** | 描述**自己嘅感受**（how we feel） | The children were **excited**. |
| **-ing adjectives** | 描述**令人有感受嘅事物**（gives the feelings） | The ride was **exciting**. |

## 📝 B. Circle the correct adjectives — 答案（學生全啱 ✅）

1. The dog is **frightening**. / The girl is **frightened**.
2. Sarah is **disappointed** / results are **disappointing**
3. The special effects are **amazing**. / The audience are **amazed**.
4. The cartoon is **amusing**. / We are **amused**.
5. The computer game is **exciting**. / Dan is **excited**.

## 📝 C. Fill in the blanks（boring, touched, amazed, surprised, interesting）— 答案（學生全啱 ✅）

1. The girls were **touched** by the film. They cried.
2. The museum is **interesting**. We can see many different old toys.
3. Anna was **surprised** at how high the mountain was.
4. My uncle was **amazed** by the news. He couldn't believe it.
5. The play was **boring**. We fell asleep when we were watching it.

## 📝 D. Ron's e-mail（改正錯誤形容詞）— 答案

1. It is ~~excited~~ → **exciting** here at camp.
2. The mountains are ~~amazed~~ → **amazing**!
3. It was ~~interested~~ → **amazing / interesting** to see the mountains in the moonlight ⚠️（學生寫法待核）
4. and I was ~~amazing~~ → **amazed**.
5. That evening we were all ~~amusing~~ → **amused** because Uncle Joe told us a lot of jokes.
6. we were ~~surprising~~ → **surprised** and frightened because we heard large animals outside our tents.
7. It is never ~~bored~~ → **boring** at camp!

## 🎯 學習重點
1. **人用 -ed，事物用 -ing**：I am excited / The game is exciting
2. 常見對：excited/exciting、amazed/amazing、amused/amusing、surprised/surprising、disappointed/disappointing、frightened/frightening、touched/touching、bored/boring、interested/interesting
3. ⚠️ 易錯：「It is exciting」（事物令人興奮）唔係「It is excited」；「I am amazed」唔係「I am amazing」
