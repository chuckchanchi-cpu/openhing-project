### 1. Grammar 2 — Connectives: after, before

**用法：**
- `after` = 在……之後（先發生 A，後發生 B）
- `before` = 在……之前（先發生 B，後發生 A）

**課文練習：** Sandy is making a video about how to make chicken pasta with cheese for her blog. Complete what she says with 'after' and 'before'.

1. _____ the pasta, you put it in the boiling water. (stir → put)
2. Check the pasta _____ you take it out of the boiling water.
3. Before, _____ the chicken, fry it in oil. (chop → fry)
4. After _____ the vegetables, she washes them.
5. Add salt and pepper to the chicken and vegetables _____ mixing them.
6. _____ everything in a big dish (before)
7. Add herbs and cream to the mixture, stir everything in a big dish

**句型結構：**
- After + V-ing, S + V. （先做……然後……）
- S + V + before + V-ing. （在……之前先做……）

### 2. Reading Skill: Making inferences（推論）

**文章：** McDonald's location in Hong Kong set to become world's busiest
（Young Post, 23 June 2025）

**重點內容：**
- Out of more than 40,000 stores worldwide, McDonald's flagship store in **Admiralty, Hong Kong** is set to become the fast-food chain's world's busiest store
- Admiralty Centre store reopened in May after a three-week renovation
- Serves more than **1,000 customers an hour** during peak periods
- Hong Kong displayed its first golden arches logo along **Paterson Street in Causeway Bay** in **January 1975**
- The city claims **four of McDonald's five busiest stores worldwide**
- Served more than **24 million customers** over the past decade: 7.5 million Chicken McNuggets, 4 million servings of French fries, 2.3 million cups of coffee
- First in Hong Kong to use **'Smart Food Lockers'** (keep food at perfect temperatures)
- New store design: **22-metre-long curved interactive LED screen**, two party spaces
- Only place in Hong Kong offering special menu items: mini choco churros, McPops doughnuts, salad burger (inspired by South Korea, France, New Zealand)
- Provides **table service**

### 3. Making Inferences 題目（MCQ）

**Q1.** According to the article, what is the most likely reason for the McDonald's store to be the world's busiest store?
- A. The store offers food that cannot be found in other stores
- B. McDonald's has a long history
- C. It is newly renovated
- D. It sells a lot of food

**Q2.** How does Randy Lai most likely feel when he says "The city now claims four of McDonald's five busiest stores worldwide"?
- A. Upset
- B. Annoyed
- C. Worried
- D. Proud

**Q3.** Why does the writer mention 'Smart Food Lockers' in the article?
- A. To show how McDonald's decorate their stores
- B. To show how McDonald's keeps food warm
- C. To show how popular the Admiralty store is
- D. To explain why delivery people will not take the wrong orders

**Q4 (開放題).** Would you like to visit the Admiralty McDonald's store? If yes, what are you most looking forward to? If no, why not?

**推論技巧：**
- 答案要引用文章中嘅句子或詞組作證據
- 注意作者用詞（如 'great achievement'、'claims'）反映人物情緒
