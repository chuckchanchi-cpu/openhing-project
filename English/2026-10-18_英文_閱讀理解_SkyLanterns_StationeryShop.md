## 📖 Part 2：The History of Sky Lanterns（Q23-28）

### 篇章重點（段落 i-iv）

| 段 | 重點 |
|----|------|
| (i) | 天燈約 **1,800 年前**由中國發明（簡單發明，唔係魔術）；相傳**諸葛亮（Zhuge Liang／Zhuge Kongming）**喺軍事上用嚟**傳遞訊息**，所以又叫 **Kongming lanterns**；現今用嚟**許願同慶祝成功** |
| (ii) | 成都天燈匠 **Eugene Yong**：年少時比賽失利嗰日見到數以百計天燈升空 → 擱置傷心返屋企學做天燈；起初常失敗（紙張 **delicate**，好易撕破）；大學畢業後以造天燈為生 |
| (iii) | 原理：燈內**熱空氣比外面冷空氣輕**所以會飛；設計**底細面大**防止熱空氣漏走；製作次序：竹枝/鐵線做底框 → 黏上**油紙**做燈殼 → 底框中央裝上可燃物（**蠟燭或紙捲**） |
| (iv) | 各地慶祝：中國大陸**一/二月元宵節**；台灣**二/三月平溪**放天燈寫願望（旅遊熱點）；日本（**八月**）、泰國（**十一月**）少數城市放天燈祈福 |

### 題目與正確答案

**Q23. Which of the following is TRUE according to paragraph 1?**
- A. Sky lanterns are a complicated invention.（❌ 簡單發明）
- B. Sky lanterns were used for celebrations in the past.（❌ 過去係軍事傳訊，現今先係慶祝）
- **C. Another name for sky lanterns is Kongming lanterns. ✅**
- D. A Chinese soldier invented sky lanterns 1,800 years ago.（❌ 諸葛亮係 strategist 軍師，唔係 soldier；「It is said」相傳）
- 💡 逐項對段一：C 直接原文對應。

**Q24. What does 'delicate' mean in paragraph 2?**
- A. small　**B. thin ✅**　C. heavy　D. thick
- 💡 上下文：「It was light... but it was easy to tear」→ 紙薄（thin）易撕破。

**Q25. According to paragraph 3, what is NOT used to make sky lanterns?（圖片題）**
- A. 鐵線圈（iron wire ✅ 有用）　**B. 燈泡（light bulb）✅**　C. 蠟燭（candle ✅ 有用）　D. 紙（rice paper ✅ 有用）
- 💡 逆向題：鐵線/蠟燭/油紙原文都有提，燈泡冇提。⚠️ 係「造天燈」用途，唔係一般照明。

**Q26. Which is TRUE about sky lanterns in different regions?**
- A. In mainland China, people do not celebrate the Lantern Festival.（❌ 有用嚟慶祝）
- B. In Japan, the whole country uses sky lanterns to wish for good luck.（❌ 只係 a few cities）
- C. In Taiwan, some cities hold a festival with sky lanterns in January.（❌ 二/三月）
- **D. In Taiwan, Pingxi is a popular tourist attraction for flying sky lanterns. ✅**
- 💡 平溪＝二/三月放天燈旅遊熱點，逐字對應。

**Q27. What is the main purpose of this article?**
- **A. to introduce facts about sky lanterns ✅**
- B. to tell readers how to make sky lanterns（只係段三部分）
- C. to tell a story about a sky lantern maker（只係段二部分）
- D. to encourage readers to release their own sky lanterns（結尾修辭問句）
- 💡 主旨題要覆蓋全文（歷史+原理+匠人+各地）→ 介紹天燈知識。⚠️ B/C/D 太窄。

**Q28. 配對題（段落標題）**
- **(i) D. History of Invention｜(ii) C. Inspiring Lights｜(iii) A. How it Works｜(iv) B. Around the World**
- 💡 (i) 發明歷史 → D；(ii) Eugene 嘅勵志故事 → C；(iii) 原理 → A；(iv) 各地 → B

## 📖 Part 3：A Stationery Shop's Journey（Q29-35）

### 篇章重點（Sam Lee 文具店 1965-退休）

| 時期 | 重點 |
|------|------|
| 1965 | Sam 七歲，父母返工時成日去區內文具店；好客店主俾佢試文具、聽佢意見、幫手 → 夢想擁有一間文具店 |
| 1982 | 中學畢業後做散工，**儲起大份人工**達成目標 → 喺新商場租鋪，平價賣文具＋賣卡通玩具；之後買咗部**大打印機**俾客人用（當時少人有打印機）→ **店鋪出名** |
| 1985-2005 | 店鋪 **blossomed**（興旺）：附近開學校、客人多；學生買平價文具、流連店鋪；考試季節 Sam 俾零食、祝好運 |
| 2010s 末 | 網購興起＋海外連鎖店 → 客少、**租金不斷加**、難以維持 → 結業；舊客人聞訊來探、買晒剩低文具 |
| 將來 | 移居**澳洲**照顧孫仔孫女；永遠珍惜店鋪嘅回憶 |

### 題目與正確答案

**Q29. In paragraph 2, what does 'goal' refer to?**
- A. saving up a lot of money　**B. opening a stationery shop ✅**　C. trying out different stationery　D. graduating from a secondary school
- 💡 段一末「I realised I hoped to have my own stationery shop one day」→ goal = 開文具店。

**Q30. Sam's shop became famous mainly because ______.**
- A. he sold stationery at a fair price　B. he sold toys from popular children's shows　**C. there was a large printer at his shop ✅**　D. he rented a shop in a new shopping mall
- 💡 「Since not many people had printers at that time, my store became well-known」→ 打印機係出名主因。

**Q31. In paragraph 3, 'blossomed' means ______.**
- A. made flowers　B. grew poorer　C. moved　**D. grew stronger ✅**
- 💡 blossomed 本義開花；上下文「more and more customers came」→ 生意興旺（grew stronger）。⚠️ 唔係字面「開花」。

**Q32. In paragraph 3, students liked to go to Sam's shop mainly because ______.**
- A. they grew up there　**B. he was nice to them ✅**　C. he gave them snacks　D. he sold branded stationery
- 💡 B 涵蓋：俾零食、祝好運、俾佢哋流連聽意見——「nice」最完整。⚠️ C 只係其中一項。

**Q33. Which is NOT the reason for Sam's retirement?**
- A. The cost of his shop increased.（✅ 租金加）
- B. Online shopping became popular.（✅）
- **C. He had to take care of his grandchildren. ✅（答案：唔係結業原因）**
- D. Big chain stores from overseas opened in Hong Kong.（✅）
- 💡 逆向題：照顧孫仔係「將來計劃」（段四末），唔係結業原因。⚠️ 時序唔好倒轉。

**Q34. In paragraph 4, Sam was glad because ______.**
- A. he was retiring　**B. his customers came to visit ✅**　C. he planned to go to Australia afterwards　D. he shared his stories about his life with his old customers
- 💡 「Surprisingly, many of my old customers came to visit me... I was glad」→ 舊客人來探 + 買晒剩低文具。⚠️ D 倒轉——係客人分享佢哋嘅故事。

**Q35. 事件排序題（p.6 answer book）**
- **C. Sam saved up a lot of money → A. Sam opened his shop → D. Sam's shop became popular → B. Sam moved to Australia**（C → A → D → B）
- 💡 時序：儲錢（1982 前）→ 開店（1982）→ 興旺（1985-2005）→ 移居澳洲（將來）。

## ✍️ Section C：Writing Q36（30 marks）— Diary Entry

**題目**：You are Helen. Last Saturday afternoon, you ran into your friend when you were on your way to choir practice. Based on the pictures below, write a diary entry about what happened. Write at least 80 words. You may use the words under the pictures to help you.

**圖片提示**：(a) afternoon tea / money（屋企見到檯面有錢，諗住下午茶）(b) hungry / thirsty（路上遇到朋友，去 Snack Shop，餐牌 A $15 / B $15 / C $20）(c) menu / order（落單：A/B/C？）(d) What happened next?（開放結局，自己諗）

**寫作要求（評分重點）**：
- 日記格式：日期、Dear Diary / 稱呼、過去式敘事
- 故事連貫：出門 → 遇友 → 小食店 → 落單 → 結局
- 至少 80 字；情節合理（餐牌價錢：$15/$15/$20，用零用錢夠唔夠？）
- 結局 (d) 自由發揮：例如請朋友食、慳錢揀平嘅、落單後發現唔夠錢、分享下午茶
