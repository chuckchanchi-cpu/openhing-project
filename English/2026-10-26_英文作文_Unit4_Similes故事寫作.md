## 📖 A. What is a simile?
- Simile = 用 'like' 或 'as' 比較兩樣有共同特質嘅事物，令描述更生動
- Examples: She is as sweet as candy. / Her eyes are as shiny as stars. / He is as proud as a peacock. / He is as slow as a sloth.

## 📝 B. Sentence Expansion（as + adjective + as）

| 原句 | Subject | Adjective | Simile | 擴寫 |
|------|---------|-----------|--------|------|
| The princess is pretty.（例） | The princess | pretty | as pretty as a flower | The princess is as pretty as a flower. |
| The princess' hair is smooth. | The princess' hair | smooth | **as smooth as silk** | The princess' hair is as smooth as silk. ✅ |
| The witch is cunning. | The witch | cunning | **as cunning as a fox** | The witch is as cunning as a fox. ✅ |
| The princess is happy. | The princess | happy | **as happy as a lark** | The princess is as happy as a lark. ✅ |

## 📖 C. 故事構思：Fiona 公主（5 圖 + 關鍵詞）

**故事開端**：Once upon a time, there was a beautiful princess called **Fiona**. One day, a witch came to the palace. She saw the princess. Then what happened?

| 圖 | 情節 | Keys words / phrases |
|----|------|---------------------|
| 1 | Once upon a time…（皇宮生活） | princess / palace / as gentle as… / as…as… |
| 2 | One day…（巫婆捉走公主困入籠）「Let me go!」「Keep quiet!」「Don't try to escape.」 | palace / catch / cage｜**用 reported speech 寫對白** |
| 3 | 巫婆畀魔法藥丸「Take the pills.」（交換面孔） | wicked / magic pills / exchange the faces / cry / beg / not listen |
| 4 | 新角色出場（變臉後？） | The king / The prince / The fairy / …turn into… / feel |
| 5 | 故事教訓 | be aware of / run away from bad person / not be greedy / be thankful / …more important than… |

**D. 分段計劃**：Paragraph 1-4 各配圖 1-4

## ✅ P.6 寫作 Checklist（評分要點）
1. ✅ **Write in four paragraphs**（四段式）
2. ✅ 第 1 段要有 **setting**（time and place）＋ **characters**
3. ✅ 全篇用**過去式**（There **was** a beautiful princess…）
4. ✅ 對白要用**正確標點**（"Help! Help!" shouted Fiona.）
5. ✅ 用 **reported speech** 轉述說話（The witch told Fiona **not to escape**.）
6. ✅ 用 **simile 'as + adjective + as'** 比較（Fiona was **as gentle as a lamb**.）
7. ✅ 傳遞**正面思想**（We learnt that we should be thankful.）

## 🎯 學習重點
1. as + **形容詞原形** + as：比較要「同範疇」（人對人、特質對特質）
2. 明喻令描述生動：pretty → as pretty as a flower（由平淡變畫面感）
3. 故事寫作三合一：similes（描寫）+ reported speech（對白轉述）+ 過去式（敘事）
4. ⚠️ 易錯：simile 中間用原形（as better as ❌）；對白標點（引號＋逗號/驚嘆號）
