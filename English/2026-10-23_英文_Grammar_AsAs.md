# 英文 — Grammar 2: as … as（Silly Sam is Brave + Practice）

> 來源：Justan Edu Discord #general（2026-10-23 上載工作紙）｜記錄：2026-10-23｜適用：小六
> 主題：**as + adjective + as** 比較結構（明喻）＋ reported speech 綜合練習

## 📖 文法重點：as + 形容詞 + as（像……一樣）

| 片語 | 意思 |
|------|------|
| as silly as a goose | 傻頭傻腦 |
| as sick as a dog | 病得很重 |
| as honest as ⚠️（待核，疑 as honest as the day is long / mirror） | 非常誠實 |
| as good as gold | 非常乖 |
| as wise as an owl | 非常聰明 |
| as busy as a bee | 非常忙碌 |
| as brave as a lion | 非常勇敢 |

## 📝 Silly Sam is Brave（答案）

1. Sam is **as silly as a goose**（印刷例句）
2. He eats the sweets and becomes **as sick as a dog**
3. He is **as honest as …** ⚠️（見上）
4. She asks Sam, "Why can't you stop doing silly things and be **as good as gold**?"
5. Tom is **as wise as an owl**. He always gets full marks in his tests.
6. She is **as busy as a bee**（追蝴蝶）→ 跌落水塘，Sam 衝去救
7. She said to him, "You were **as brave as a lion**! I'm very proud of you!"

## 📝 Practice（Reported speech 綜合）

### Cinderella（間接引語改寫）
- Cinderella's stepsisters **told her to clean** the kitchen. They **told her not to leave** the house.（told + 人 + (not) to + 原形）
- She was **as gentle as a lamb**（溫順得像羔羊）
- Fairy Godmother **asked Cinderella to get ready** for the ball. She made Cinderella look **as beautiful as a princess**.
- Before she left, Fairy Godmother **asked her to come back before twelve o'clock**.

### Little Red Riding Hood（直接 → 間接引語）
| 直接引語 | 間接引語 |
|----------|----------|
| "Please take some cakes to your grandma."（ask） | Mother **asked her to take** some cakes to her grandma. |
| "Don't speak to strangers."（tell） | She **told Little Red Riding Hood not to speak** to strangers. |
| "Please pick some flowers for your grandma."（ask） | Big Bad Wolf **asked Little Red Riding Hood to pick** some flowers for her grandma. |
| "Don't hurry."（tell） | He **told her not to hurry**. |

## 🎯 學習重點
1. as + adj + as：形容詞用**原形**（as good as ✅ / as better as ❌）
2. 明喻片語要成對記（goose/sick dog/gold/owl/bee/lion）
3. Please… → ask；Don't… → tell + not to（同前日 reported speech 呼應）
