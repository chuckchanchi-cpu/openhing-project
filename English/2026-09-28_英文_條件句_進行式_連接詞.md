## 一、Grammar Focus：Conditional Sentences（if 條件句）

**規則：** `If + 簡單現在式 (simple present), ... + 簡單未來式 (will + 原形)`
- If clause（條件）用**現在式**
- Result clause（結果）用**未來式 will**

例句：*If Dad **comes** home early, we **will watch** a film.*

### Activity 1 答案
| 題 | 答案 | 說明 |
|----|------|------|
| 1. If you ___ (go) to see this film, you will have a good time. | **go** | if 子句用現在式 |
| 2. If he ___ (play) sport, he will live longer. | **plays** | he + 現在式加 s |
| 3. She ___ (not be) an architect if she doesn't go to university. | **will not be / won't be** | 結果子句用 will |
| 4. They ___ (ring) us if we give them our phone number. | **will ring** | 結果子句用 will |
| 5. If we ___ (not solve) the problem, we won't get the prize. | **don't solve** | if 子句用現在式 |

- 💡 技巧：見到 **if** → 嗰半句用**現在式**；另一半（結果）先至用 **will**。
- ⚠️ 陷阱：**千祈唔好在 if 後面加 will**！`If you will go` ❌ → `If you go` ✅

## 二、Verbs：現在進行式 vs 簡單現在式

**分別：**
- **is/am/are + V-ing** → 而家正在做（happening now）
- **V / V-s** → 習慣、常做（every day / every Saturday / always）

### Activity B 對話答案（Chow 一家人）
| 空格 | 答案 | 線索 |
|------|------|------|
| (1) He ___ (walk) the dog. | **is walking** | 而家做緊（What is the father doing?） |
| (2) He ___ (walk) the dog every afternoon. | **walks** | every afternoon = 習慣 |
| (3) She ___ (go) to the supermarket. | **is going** | 而家做緊 |
| (4) She ___ (do) the shopping every Saturday. | **does** | every Saturday = 習慣 |
| (5) They ___ (be) always helpful. | **are** | always + be，狀態用現在式 |
| (6) She ___ (have) dancing lessons on Saturdays. | **has** | on Saturdays = 習慣 |
| (7) ___ she ___ (have) dancing lessons every week? | **Does … have** | 現在式問句用 Does + 原形 |
| (8) She ___ (come) home at four o'clock every Saturday. | **comes** | every Saturday = 習慣 |
| (9) She ___ (be) never late. | **is** | never + be，用現在式 |

- 💡 技巧：搵**時間詞**決定時態 —— `now / Look!` → 進行式；`every day / always / on Saturdays / never` → 簡單現在式。
- ⚠️ 陷阱1：(7) 問句 `Does she have` —— Does 後面用**原形 have**，唔係 having/has。
- ⚠️ 陷阱2：(9) `is never late`（late 係形容詞，前面用 be 動詞 is），唔好寫成 `is being never late`。
- ⚠️ 陷阱3：(5)(9) 有 always/never 但動詞係 **be**，直接用 are/is，唔使加 -ing。

## 三、Connectives：so / so that / because

**分別（最易錯！）：**
| 連接詞 | 功能 | 後面接 | 例子 |
|--------|------|--------|------|
| **because** | 原因（因為） | 原因 | I stayed home **because** it rained. |
| **so** | 結果（所以） | 結果 | It rained, **so** I stayed home. |
| **so that** | 目的（以便、為了） | can/will + 目的 | I studied hard **so that** I **can** pass. |

### Activity E 電郵答案
| 空格 | 答案 | 原因/結果/目的 |
|------|------|----------------|
| (1) It was my birthday yesterday ___ we had a big meal. | **so** | 結果 |
| (2) I will help Mum ___ she will not be so busy. | **so that** | 目的 |
| (3) I like singing ___ I will join the choir. | **so** | 結果 |
| (4) I will practise every day ___ I can win the contest. | **so that** | 目的（有 can） |
| (5) He did not play ___ he was sick. | **because** | 原因 |
| (6) He was very upset ___ it was the last match. | **because** | 原因 |
| (7) He has to study hard ___ he will not fail it. | **so that** | 目的 |

### Activity F 改寫句子答案
1. Bobby felt cold. He switched off the fan.
   → **Bobby felt cold, so he switched off the fan.**
2. We have to be very careful. We don't want to make any mistakes.
   → **We have to be very careful because we don't want to make any mistakes.**（或 …so that we won't make any mistakes.）
3. We put on suntan lotion. We do not get sunburn.
   → **We put on suntan lotion so that we do not get sunburn.**
4. Riding a horse is fun. It is dangerous.
   → **Riding a horse is fun but it is dangerous.**

- 💡 技巧：見到後句有 **can / will + 想做某事** → 多係**目的** → 用 so that；講**已發生嘅原因**用 because；講**因此產生嘅結果**用 so。
- ⚠️ 陷阱：so 前面通常加**逗號**（結果）；so that 前面通常**無逗號**（目的）。
