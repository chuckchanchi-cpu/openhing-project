# 🌕 Mooncake Maths — English Practice (A Helping Hand vibe)

> The EnergyPedia editor flew Chang'e to the Moon. Now let's help **her** help **others** — because a true helping hand raises more than money. 😎

（⚠️ All grammar is from your Unit 1 context: `so`, `who/which`, charity vocab.）

## ✺ Part A · Join with "so"
1. Chang'e wants to help lonely people. → She visits an elderly home.
   - ✅ *Chang'e wants to help lonely people, **so** she visits an elderly home.*
   - 💡 技巧：原因 + **,** + so + 結果
   - ⚠️ 陷阱：千祈唔好漏咗 so 前面個**逗號**！

2. Ben cares about animals. → He adopts a pet from the SPCA.
   - ✅ *Ben cares about animals, **so** he adopts a pet from the SPCA.*

## ✺ Part B · who or which?
1. The SPCA is a charity ___ saves animals. → **which** (charity = thing)
2. A volunteer is a person ___ helps without pay. → **who** (person)
3. The mooncake ___ Chang'e eats has 800 kcal. → **which** (mooncake = thing)
4. Children ___ need our help should be loved. → **who** (children = people)

- 💡 技巧：睇空格前面嗰個詞係**人**定**物**。人→who，物→which。

## ✺ Part C · Vocabulary (Vocab-From-Text drill)
> Fill with the right word from the box.

**Word Bank:** adopt · donate · charity · volunteer · fund-raising · lonely

1. We can __________ money to help the homeless. → **donate**
2. An animal shelter cares for __________ pets. → **stray** *(延伸)*
3. Many kind people like to be a __________. → **volunteer**
4. She feels __________, so we visit her. → **lonely**
5. Our school held a __________ fair. → **fund-raising**

## ✺ Part D · Mini writing (1 paragraph)
Prompt: *What would you do to help others?*
Model: *I would like to visit a hospital **which** is near my school, **so** I can play games with sick children **who** need cheering up. Small actions can make a big difference.*

- 💡 技巧：一句入面同時用埋 so + which + who，即係滿分示範。
