# 英文 — Monkey Molly and Lion Leo＋Dad's pasta dish（故事整合：reported speech・similes・after/before）

> 來源：Justan Edu Discord #general（2026-11-01 上載工作紙）｜記錄：2026-11-01｜適用：小六
> C：用 reported speech＋as…as 完成故事｜D：Dad's pasta dish 網誌（圈正確詞）

## 📝 C. Monkey Molly and Lion Leo（word bank：brave/lion、gentle/lamb、happy/bird）

1. Mum asked Molly **to pick** some fruit in the forest.（ask + 人 + to + 原形）
2. Molly was **as happy as a bird** ✅（印刷）
3. Leo：「Please help me take the glass out of my paw.」→ Molly **helped him take the glass out of his paw**（改寫為過去式敘述）
4. Leo：「Keep still.」→（Leo/Molly）**told each other to keep still**（⚠️ 照樣改 reported speech）
5. 「Don't worry!」→ Molly told Leo **not to worry**（told + 人 + not to + 原形）
6. 「Leo, you're **as gentle as a lamb**!」（word bank）
7. Molly told them **not to be scared / (that) Leo was gentle**（⚠️ reported speech）
8. 「Leo is **as brave as a lion**.」（word bank）

**Word bank 對照**：happy/bird（②）、gentle/lamb（⑥）、brave/lion（⑧）

## 📝 D. Dad's pasta dish（圈正確詞）

1. Mum asked Dad **to make** a pasta dish.（make / making / to make）
2. Sometimes Dad is **as silly as a goose** ⚠️（as gentle as a lamb / as silly as a goose — 學生情境：跟住大聲音樂亂舞 → silly）
3. **Although** he isn't a good dancer, he loves dancing very much!（After / Although）
4. Dad asked me to help **him**.（he / him / his）
5. 雖然佢煮嘢唔係好掂，但好識教人煮 → **Although** he isn't very good at cooking, he's good at telling other people how to cook!
6. 'Boil the water **before** you add the pasta.'（after / although / before）
7. 'Add some salt and pepper **before** you mix everything together.' ⚠️
8. 'Sprinkle some cheese on the pasta **before** you put it into the oven.'
9. There was a lot to do and I was **as busy as a bee**.
10. She asked Dad to give **her** the recipe.（her / hers / she）
11. He asked Mum **to dance** with him, but he told me **to do** the washing up!（dance/dancing/to dance；do/doing/to do）

## 🎯 學習重點
1. **reported speech 三式**：ask/tell + 人 + to ＋原形；否定 not to；代詞轉（him/her）
2. **similes 三寶**：happy/bird、gentle/lamb、brave/lion（＋silly/goose、busy/bee）
3. **after/before 睇步驟先後**：boil water → add pasta → season → mix → cheese → oven
4. ⚠️ 易錯：asked me to help **him**（唔係 his）；although 唔加 but；步驟詞唔好調轉
