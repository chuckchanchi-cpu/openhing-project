## 文章

Kandy is a large city located in the central part of Sri Lanka. It is 115 km away from Colombo. The city was the last capital of the Sri Lankan kings. It is surrounded by hills and the Mahaweli River. Kandy is also a World Heritage Site. There are many cultural and religious sites in Kandy. One of the most famous attractions is the Temple of the Tooth Relic. It houses a tooth relic of the Buddha. Every year, there is a grand festival called the Esala Perahera. During the festival, dancers and musicians parade the streets. Elephants decorated with colourful costumes join the parade too. Kandy is truly a city of culture and religion.

### Q5（詞義）
