## 文章

There is a dangerous fungus called Candida auris. It is so small that we cannot see it with our eyes. It can live on human skin without causing any problems, but it can also cause very serious infections.

Recently, scientists found a new kind of C. auris in Kenya. It was found in a 28-year-old woman. Doctors tried 13 different medicines, but her infection did not go away. This new kind of the fungus can fight against every known drug.

C. auris is not new. Scientists first found it in Japan in 2009. Since then, it has spread to 40 countries. It can live on bedrails, door handles and medical equipment in hospitals, so it spreads easily there.

People who are very sick in hospitals are most at risk. Healthy people are usually safe, because intact skin is a strong shield. But tubes and lines in hospitals can give the fungus a way into the body. If it gets into the blood, it can be life-threatening.

Doctors say we should not panic. They can fight the fungus with good hygiene, such as cleaning surfaces, screening patients and isolating cases. The hardest part is finding it early, because many laboratories cannot identify yeasts.

## 閱讀理解（8 題・四選一）

### Q1（主旨）
This passage is mainly about:
- A. how to clean a hospital
- B. a dangerous drug-resistant fungus and the risks it brings
- C. a woman who lives in Kenya
- D. how to make new medicines

**答案：** B
**文中依據：** 首段介紹 C. auris ＋全文講抗藥性、傳播、風險與應對。
**💡技巧：** 主旨覆蓋全文，唔係一段。
**⚠️陷阱：** C 只係第一宗個案人物。

### Q2（細節）
Where did scientists first find C. auris?
- A. Kenya
- B. Japan
- C. Singapore
- D. London

**答案：** B
**文中依據：** 「Scientists first found it in Japan in 2009.」
**💡技巧：** 「first found」＋地名掃描。
**⚠️陷阱：** Kenya 係最新個案，唔係首次發現。

### Q3（細節）
How many medicines did doctors try on the woman in Kenya?
- A. 2
- B. 13
- C. 40
- D. 2009

**答案：** B
**文中依據：** 「Doctors tried 13 different medicines」
**💡技巧：** 數字題掃描「medicines」。
**⚠️陷阱：** 40 係國家數。

### Q4（推論）
Why are sick people in hospitals most at risk?
- A. because hospital food is bad
- B. because tubes and lines can give the fungus a way into the body
- C. because hospitals are dark
- D. because they walk too much

**答案：** B
**文中依據：** 「tubes and lines in hospitals can give the fungus a way into the body」。
**💡技巧：** 推論睇「entry point」解釋。
**⚠️陷阱：** A/C/D 無中生有。

### Q5（詞義）
In the passage, "shield" means:
- A. something that protects
- B. a kind of medicine
- C. a hospital room
- D. a germ

**答案：** A
**文中依據：** 「intact skin is a strong shield」— 皮膚係保護屏障。
**💡技巧：** 後文「gives the fungus a way in」＝shield 擋住。
**⚠️陷阱：** shield 原意係盾牌 →「保護」。

### Q6（逆向・NOT）
Which of the following is NOT mentioned as a way to fight the fungus?
- A. cleaning surfaces
- B. screening patients
- C. isolating cases
- D. taking vitamins

**答案：** D
**文中依據：** 「good hygiene, such as cleaning surfaces, screening patients and isolating cases」。
**💡技巧：** NOT 題逐項對照。
**⚠️陷阱：** A/B/C 全部原文有提。

### Q7（因果/目的）
Why is it hard to find C. auris early?
- A. because it only comes out at night
- B. because many laboratories cannot identify yeasts
- C. because it is too big to see
- D. because it hides in food

**答案：** B
**文中依據：** 「The hardest part is finding it early, because many laboratories cannot identify yeasts.」
**💡技巧：** 因果題搵「because」直接答案。
**⚠️陷阱：** C 剛好相反（佢係太細睇唔到）。

### Q8（作者態度/深層意思）
What do doctors say about the fungus?

- B. we should not panic, but we must find it early

- D. it will go away by itself

**答案：** B
**文中依據：** 「Doctors say we should not panic」＋「The hardest part is finding it early」。
**💡技巧：** 態度題睇「should」句式。
**⚠️陷阱：** C 同「life-threatening」矛盾。
