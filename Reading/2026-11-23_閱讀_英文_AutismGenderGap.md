**Gene may explain autism gender gap**

Autism may be less common in women and girls because of just one gene on the X chromosome, discovers **Michael Marshall**

The X chromosome houses a striking set of genes that can have a disproportionate effect on a set of traits that contribute to the risk of autism. As these genes are silenced in women but not in men, this may help explain why autism is less common in females.

Many studies have found autism is at least three times more common in males than in females, up to four times as many in males, says Anders Andreassen at Boston Children's Hospital in Massachusetts. There is a genetic component to this. "We have long known that there is a strong female protective component of autism," he says.

Nevertheless, many autism researchers think there is something about being female makes people less likely to be diagnosed with autism – and this is linked to have more autism-linked mutations than men diagnosed with autism. This suggests "there is a required genetic load in females in order to manifest to a degree to a level that would be diagnosed," says Andreassen. "We have long known that there is a strong female protective component of autism."

"Unusually for genes on the X chromosomes, both copies of the gene are expressed in females," he says. "There have been a lot of large-scale sequencing studies that have identified hundreds of genes implicated in autism," he says.

To figure out how these genes work, Andreassen and his colleagues grew neural progenitor cells – which develop into neurons – and then targeted 29 genes, all known to affect the chances of developing autism. They used CRISPR gene-editing technology to break down the RNA produced by these genes, in order to reduce the expression of these genes.

Reduced expression of these genes in females lessened their chances of developing autism. "We could target the RNA in females with a reduced expression level of those genes," says Andreassen. The team found that reducing the expression of any one of these genes tended to reduce the expression of most of the others. "If it was just a single gene, we would have to converge in some way, in order to be related to the rest of the network," says Andreassen. The model of the interactions between the genes enabled them to identify a gene that strongly regulates the autism-associated genes. "This provides strong evidence that the same group of genes causes of autism may converge," he says. "This suggests ZFX could act as a buffer. A girl could have mutations in other high-risk autism-associated genes and then increase the expression of those genes and reduce her chances of developing autism."

The team then identified a set of 86 genes that were expressed less when the autism-related version of ZFX was active. Many of these were known to be more highly expressed in people with autism. This led the researchers to ZFX. Females have two X chromosomes, and males have one X and a smaller Y. So females have two copies of these genes – and unusually for genes on the X chromosome, both copies of the genes are expressed in females. The researchers report in Nature, doi.org/k44n. A girl could have mutations in other high-risk autism-associated genes and then increase the expression of those genes and reduce her chances of developing autism," says Andreassen.

## 閱讀理解（8 題・四選一）

### Q1（主旨）
This article is mainly about:
- A. how CRISPR can cure autism
- B. a gene on the X chromosome that may explain why autism is less common in females
- C. the life of Michael Marshall
- D. how to diagnose autism in hospitals

**答案：** B
**文中依據：** 標題「Gene may explain autism gender gap」＋副題「Autism may be less common in women and girls because of just one gene on the X chromosome」
**💡技巧：** 主旨題睇標題＋副題，佢哋已經講晒全文重心。
**⚠️陷阱：** A「cure」— 文中只係研究機理，冇講治愈；C 只係作者名。

### Q2（細節）
According to studies, autism is at least how many times more common in males than in females?
- A. two times
- B. three times
- C. four times
- D. ten times

**答案：** B
**文中依據：** 「autism is at least three times more common in males than in females」
**💡技巧：** 數字題掃描「times」。
**⚠️陷阱：** 「up to four times」係上限，題目問「at least」＝最少三倍。

### Q3（細節）
Who is Anders Andreassen?
- A. a researcher at Boston Children's Hospital in Massachusetts
- B. a teacher at Trinity College Dublin
- C. a doctor at Yale School of Medicine
- D. a journalist at New Scientist

**答案：** A
**文中依據：** 「says Anders Andreassen at Boston Children's Hospital in Massachusetts」
**💡技巧：** 人名＋機構名係好錨點。
**⚠️陷阱：** Gallagher（都柏林）、Krystal（耶魯）係其他人嘅機構。

### Q4（推論）
Why do females have a "protective" advantage against autism?
- A. because they have two X chromosomes and both copies of the gene are expressed
- B. because they have two Y chromosomes
- C. because CRISPR protects them
- D. because they have fewer genes

**答案：** A
**文中依據：** 「Females have two X chromosomes… both copies of the genes are expressed in females… ZFX could act as a buffer.」
**💡技巧：** 推論題連接兩個事實：兩份 X＋兩份基因表達＝保護。
**⚠️陷阱：** B 事實錯誤（女性冇 Y）；C CRISPR 係研究工具，唔係保護機制。

### Q5（詞義）
The word "buffer" in "ZFX could act as a buffer" most nearly means:
- A. something that reduces or softens an effect
- B. a type of chromosome
- C. a medicine
- D. a laboratory tool

**答案：** A
**文中依據：** 「A girl could have mutations… and then increase the expression of those genes and reduce her chances of developing autism」— buffer 係緩衝、減低影響。
**💡技巧：** 詞義題睇後文解釋句。
**⚠️陷阱：** B/D 係文中其他概念（染色體/實驗工具），唔係 buffer 嘅意思。

### Q7（因果/目的）
Why did the researchers use CRISPR gene-editing technology?
- A. to create new genes
- B. to reduce the expression of the targeted genes by breaking down their RNA
- C. to change the sex of the cells
- D. to make the cells grow faster

**答案：** B
**文中依據：** 「They used CRISPR gene-editing technology to break down the RNA produced by these genes, in order to reduce the expression of these genes.」
**💡技巧：** 目的題搵「in order to」句式。
**⚠️陷阱：** CRISPR 常被誤解為「改造基因」— 本文用嚟「減少基因表達」。
