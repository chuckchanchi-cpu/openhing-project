## 文章

Autism is a condition that affects how a person thinks and talks with others. Doctors have found that autism is more common in boys than in girls. For every girl with autism, there are at least three boys with it. But why?

Scientists like Anders Andreassen at Boston Children's Hospital want to find the answer. They found something interesting about a gene called ZFX. This gene sits on the X chromosome. Girls have two X chromosomes, so they have two copies of the ZFX gene. Boys have only one.

ZFX works like a shield. When the ZFX gene works well, it can lower the chance of developing autism. In girls, the two copies of the gene can protect them. In boys, there is only one copy, so there is less protection.

To study this, the scientists used a special tool called CRISPR to change genes in cells. They found that ZFX helps control many other genes that are linked to autism.

However, not all scientists agree. John Krystal says more tests are needed. Louise Gallagher says autism is complex, and one gene cannot explain everything.

## 閱讀理解（8 題・四選一）

### Q1（主旨）
This passage is mainly about:
- A. how doctors treat sick children
- B. why autism is more common in boys than in girls
- C. what CRISPR can do in hospitals
- D. the life of a scientist

**答案：** B
**文中依據：** 首段問題「But why?」＋全文解釋 ZFX 基因與性別差異。
**💡技巧：** 主旨題睇首段嘅核心問題。
**⚠️陷阱：** A/C 只係文中一角。

### Q2（細節）
For every girl with autism, how many boys have it?
- A. at least two
- B. at least three
- C. at least four
- D. only one

**答案：** B
**文中依據：** 「For every girl with autism, there are at least three boys with it.」
**💡技巧：** 數字題掃描「three」。
**⚠️陷阱：** 「two X chromosomes」係另一個數字，唔好撞。

### Q3（細節）
What is the name of the gene that may protect girls?
- A. ZFX
- B. CRISPR
- C. DNA
- D. X-chrom

**答案：** A
**文中依據：** 「a gene called ZFX… In girls, the two copies of the gene can protect them.」
**💡技巧：** 專有名詞（大階）快掃。
**⚠️陷阱：** CRISPR 係工具唔係基因。

### Q4（推論）
Why do girls have more protection against autism?
- A. because they eat better food
- B. because they have two copies of the ZFX gene
- C. because they have no X chromosome
- D. because doctors protect them

**答案：** B
**文中依據：** 「Girls have two X chromosomes, so they have two copies of the ZFX gene.」
**💡技巧：** 推論題連接「兩個 X」＋「保護」兩個事實。
**⚠️陷阱：** C 事實錯誤。

### Q5（詞義）
In the passage, "shield" means:
- A. something that protects
- B. a kind of food
- C. a school subject
- D. a machine

**答案：** A
**文中依據：** 「ZFX works like a shield… it can lower the chance of developing autism」— shield＝保護。
**💡技巧：** 詞義題睇後文解釋（lower the chance）。
**⚠️陷阱：** shield 原意係盾牌 — 文中用作比喻「保護」。

### Q6（逆向・NOT）
Which of the following is NOT mentioned in the passage?
- A. CRISPR
- B. Boston Children's Hospital
- C. a vaccine against autism
- D. the X chromosome

**答案：** C
**文中依據：** A/B/D 全部出現；文中新冇提 vaccine。
**💡技巧：** NOT 題逐項對照原文。
**⚠️陷阱：** 唔好因為「醫生」就聯想 vaccine。

### Q7（因果/目的）
Why did the scientists use CRISPR?
- A. to change genes in cells and study how they work
- B. to build new hospitals
- C. to make boys stronger
- D. to draw pictures of genes

**答案：** A
**文中依據：** 「they used a special tool called CRISPR to change genes in cells」＋目的係研究 ZFX。
**💡技巧：** 目的題搵「to…」不定式。
**⚠️陷阱：** C/D 無中生有。

### Q8（作者態度/深層意思）
What do Krystal and Gallagher think about the finding?
- A. they think it is completely wrong
- B. they think it is interesting but more tests are needed
- C. they think the study is perfect
- D. they did not say anything

**答案：** B
**文中依據：** 「Krystal says more tests are needed. Gallagher says autism is complex, and one gene cannot explain everything.」
**💡技巧：** 態度題睇最後一段嘅保留語氣。
**⚠️陷阱：** A 太極端；兩位科學家係「保留」唔係「反對」。
