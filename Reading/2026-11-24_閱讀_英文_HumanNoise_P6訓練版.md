## 文章

Human noise is a big problem for animals. Very few places on Earth are quiet. In the ocean, the noise level has doubled every ten years since 1950.

Recently, scientists studied 177 kinds of animals. They found that noise changed the lives of 80 per cent of them, and the changes were always bad. "Every habitat is affected by noise," says Helmut Kunc, one of the scientists.

Noise affects animals in many ways. First, it is harder for them to find food. In noisy places, predators must work 2.5 times harder to catch 25 per cent less food. Second, noise disrupts communication. Whales and dolphins use sound to find their way, so ship engines confuse them. Third, animals avoid noisy places. When animals leave their homes, their living areas become smaller, and their numbers fall.

Noise can also kill. Sometimes animals die quickly from loud noise, such as blasts from oil and gas exploration. Sometimes they die slowly, because they cannot catch enough food.

A few predators do benefit from noise, because noise drives their prey out of hiding. But Kunc says the overall effect is still bad. He wants people to think about the soundscape of nature when they protect plants and animals. Quiet is important for life.

## 閱讀理解（8 題・四選一）

### Q1（主旨）
This passage is mainly about:
- A. how ships are built
- B. how human noise harms animals in many ways
- C. how whales sing
- D. how to find food in the ocean

**答案：** B
**文中依據：** 首段「Human noise is a big problem for animals」＋全文列舉影響。
**💡技巧：** 主旨題睇首段主題句。
**⚠️陷阱：** C 只係其中一個例子。

### Q2（細節）
How much has the ocean noise level risen every ten years since 1950?
- A. it has doubled
- B. it has stayed the same
- C. it has halved
- D. it has tripled

**答案：** A
**文中依據：** 「the noise level has doubled every ten years since 1950」
**💡技巧：** 數字題掃描「since 1950」。
**⚠️陷阱：** 「2.5 times」「25 per cent」係其他數字。

### Q3（細節）
How many kinds of animals did the scientists study?
- A. 80
- B. 177
- C. 1950
- D. 25

**答案：** B
**文中依據：** 「scientists studied 177 kinds of animals」
**💡技巧：** 數字題搵「studied」後面嘅數。
**⚠️陷阱：** 80 係百分比，唔係數量。

### Q4（推論）
Why do the numbers of some animals fall?
- A. because they eat too much
- B. because they leave noisy places, so their living areas become smaller
- C. because they grow too fast
- D. because people feed them

**答案：** B
**文中依據：** 「When animals leave their homes, their living areas become smaller, and their numbers fall.」
**💡技巧：** 推論連接「離開」＋「面積變小」＋「數量跌」。
**⚠️陷阱：** A/D 無中生有。

### Q5（詞義）
The word "disrupts" in "noise disrupts communication" means:
- A. breaks or disturbs
- B. helps
- C. records
- D. hides

**答案：** A
**文中依據：** 鯨豚用聲音認路，船聲「confuse them」→ 打擾/破壞通訊。
**💡技巧：** 詞義題睇後文解釋（confuse）。
**⚠️陷阱：** 唔好答「helps」— 全文係負面。

### Q6（逆向・NOT）
Which of the following is NOT mentioned as an effect of noise?
- A. it is harder for animals to find food
- B. noise confuses whales and dolphins
- C. noise makes animals run faster than planes
- D. animals die slowly because they cannot catch enough food

**答案：** C
**文中依據：** A/B/D 全部有提；「跑得快過飛機」無中生有。
**💡技巧：** NOT 題逐項對照。
**⚠️陷阱：** 其他三項都係文中真實影響。

### Q7（因果/目的）
Why do a few predators benefit from noise?
- A. because noise drives their prey out of hiding
- B. because noise makes them bigger
- C. because noise gives them wings
- D. because they like music

**答案：** A
**文中依據：** 「noise drives their prey out of hiding」
**💡技巧：** 因果題搵「because」直接答案。
**⚠️陷阱：** 呢個只係少數情況，整體仍然有害。

### Q8（作者態度/深層意思）
What does Kunc want people to do?
- A. make more noise
- B. think about the soundscape of nature when protecting plants and animals
- C. move animals to cities
- D. stop studying animals

**答案：** B
**文中依據：** 「He wants people to think about the soundscape of nature when they protect plants and animals.」
**💡技巧：** 主張題睇「wants」句。
**⚠️陷阱：** A 同文章立場相反；結尾「Quiet is important for life」＝要保護寧靜。
