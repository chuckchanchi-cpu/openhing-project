# 英文文法 — Simple Present Tense vs Present Continuous Tense（學習與訓練）

> 教材日期：2026-09-16｜適用：小六

## 一、Simple Present Tense（一般現在式）

### 用法
1. 談論習慣（habits）— I go to school every day.
2. 談論現在狀態（present states）— My mother is a teacher.
3. 談論事實真理（truths）— Plants die without sunlight and water.
4. 表達感受和意見（feelings and opinions）— I like ice cream.
5. 談論可能性（possibilities）— If it rains, we will stay at home.

### 動詞形式
| 主語 | 動詞形式 | 例子 |
|------|---------|------|
| I / You / We / They | 原形動詞 | They talk loudly. / I go to school. |
| He / She / It | 動詞 + s / es | She talks loudly. / He goes to school. |

### 串法規則（He / She / It）
- 一般動詞：直接加 **s** — talks, plays
- 尾音是 ch, sh, ss, o, x, z：加 **es** — watches, washes, tidies
- 子音 + y：改 y 為 **ies** — tidy → tidies
- have → **has**

### 時間詞（Signal Words）
every day, in the morning, at night, on Sundays, on Mondays, on Wednesdays

## 二、Present Continuous Tense（現在進行式）

### 用法
1. 說話時正在發生的事（happening at the time of speaking）— Tom is watching TV now.
2. 描述圖片中看到正在進行的動作（actions you see in pictures）— The girl is playing the guitar and the boys are singing in the picture.
3. 已安排的未來計劃（arrangements/plans in the future）— Aunt Lily is visiting us next Sunday.

### 動詞形式
| 主語 | 動詞形式 | 例子 |
|------|---------|------|
| He / She / It | is + 動詞ing | He is running in the park. |
| I | am + 動詞ing | I am singing now. |
| You / We / They | are + 動詞ing | They are swimming in the pool. |

### 串法規則（加 ing）
- 一般動詞：直接加 **ing** — talking, playing
- 子音 + 母音結尾（雙寫尾字母）：**double the consonant + ing** — run → running, swim → swimming, sit → sitting
- 尾音 e（去 e）：**drop the e + ing** — come → coming, leave → leaving
- 尾音 ie（改 y）：**ie → y + ing** — lie → lying, tie → tying

### 時間詞（Signal Words）
right now, at the moment, at present, Look!, Listen!

## 三、練習（Fill in the blanks）

### Exercise A：用正確的 Simple Present 形式填空
1. She ________ (jog) every night.
2. I ________ (look) after my little brother every day.
3. My mother ________ (be) a teacher.
4. Kitty ________ (like) ice cream.
5. I am worried because my mum ________ (look) angry.
6. Dad ________ (tidy) the flat every day.
7. Tom ________ (live) in Yuen Long.
8. Amy ________ (not like) dancing.
9. I usually ________ (go) to the swimming pool.
10. Peter usually ________ (visit) his grandparents.

### Exercise B：Raymond Chan 短文
Raymond Chan (1) ________ (be) a primary six student. He (2) ________ (go) to Cub Scouts on Saturday afternoon and he (3) ________ (study) with his classmates every Sunday morning. He (4) ________ (like) football. On Sunday evening he (5) ________ (play) football. Afterwards he (6) ________ (watch) TV. And he (7) ________ (study) English. At about ten o'clock he (8) ________ (hug) his parents and (9) ________ (kiss) them goodnight.

### Exercise C：看圖對話（Bob 和 Jack 看 Chow 一家）
- Bob: What is the father doing?
- Jack: He (1) ________ (walk) the dog. He (2) ________ (walk) the dog every morning.
- Bob: What is the mother doing?
- Jack: She (3) ________ (go) to the supermarket. She (4) ________ (do) the shopping every Saturday.
- Bob: Can you see the boys?
- Jack: Yes, I can. They are going with her. They (5) ________ (be) always helpful.
- Bob: How about the girl?
- Jack: I don't see her. She (6) ________ (have) dancing lessons on Saturdays.
- Bob: Is she (7) ________ (have) dancing lessons this week?
- Jack: Yes, she does. She will be back soon. She (8) ________ (come) home now. She (9) ________ (be) never late.

## 四、參考答案

### Exercise A
1. jogs　2. look　3. is　4. likes　5. looks　6. tidies　7. lives　8. does not like（doesn't like）　9. go　10. visits

### Exercise B

### Exercise C
1. is walking　2. walks　3. is going　4. does　5. are　6. has　7. having　8. is coming　9. is

## 五、考試貼士
- 見到 every day / on Sundays / usually → 用 **Simple Present**
- 見到 now / at the moment / Look! / Listen! → 用 **Present Continuous**
- 圖片題：睇緊「相中動作」用進行式；「平時習慣」用現在式
- 唔好漏咗 He/She/It 嘅 s / es！
