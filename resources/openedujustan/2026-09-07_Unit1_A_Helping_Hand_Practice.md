## Part 2: Grammar 1 — Reason and Result (so)

**Remember:** We use **so** to join a reason and a result. Add a **comma (,) before so**.

Example: *We have adopted a pet from the SPCA recently, **so** I hope you can help the SPCA.*

## Part 3: Grammar 2 — who / which

**Remember:**
- Use **who** for people.
- Use **which** for animals and things.

Examples:
- *She helps people **who** have no home.*
- *The SPCA is an animal charity **which** helps and saves animals.*
