## 一、Grammar 1: so（原因和結果）

### ✨ 核心規則
**結構：** [原因] + , + **so** + [結果]

**重點：**
- **so** 連接兩個句子，表示「所以」
- **原因前面要加逗號 (,)**
- **so 後面接結果**

### 📖 課文範例（P.7）

| 例句 | 分析 |
|------|------|
| We have adopted a pet from the SPCA recently, **so** I hope you can help the SPCA. | 原因：我們領養了一隻寵物 → 結果：希望你能幫助SPCA |
| I want to help the homeless, **so** I help prepare meals for them sometimes. | 原因：想幫助無家可歸者 → 結果：有時幫他們準備食物 |
| I like hiking and helping others, **so** I will go on a walkathon in a country park soon. | 原因：喜歡遠足和助人 → 結果：將參加行山籌款活動 |
| I hope I can collect money to help blind people, **so** I will sell flags next month. | 原因：想籌款幫助盲人 → 結果：下個月賣旗 |
| I would like to help old people, **so** I visit an elderly home every month. | 原因：想幫助老人 → 結果：每月探訪老人院 |

## 二、Grammar 2: who / which（關係代詞）

### ✨ 核心規則

| 代詞 | 用法 | 例子 |
|------|------|------|
| **who** | 指**人** (people) | She helps people **who** have no home. |
| **which** | 指**物/動物** (things/animals) | The SPCA is an animal charity **which** helps and saves animals. |

### 📖 課文範例（P.8）

#### 組合句子練習：

| 原句 | 組合後 | 說明 |
|------|--------|------|
| We can support a food charity. It prepares food for people in need. | We can support a food charity **which** prepares food for people in need. | charity = 物 → 用 **which** |
| We can visit hospitals and play games with children. They are sick. | We can visit hospitals and play games with children **who** are sick. | children = 人 → 用 **who** |
| We can visit a centre. It takes care of old people. | We can visit a centre **which** takes care of old people. | centre = 物 → 用 **which** |
| We can give our old clothes and toys to boys and girls. They need them. | We can give our old clothes and toys to boys and girls **who** need them. | boys and girls = 人 → 用 **who** |

## 四、易錯點提醒

### ⚠️ 錯誤1：逗號遺漏
> ❌ We have adopted a pet from the SPCA recently so I hope you can help the SPCA.  
> ✅ We have adopted a pet from the SPCA recently, **so** I hope you can help the SPCA.  
> 💡 **原因前一定要加逗號！**

### ⚠️ 錯誤2：who/which 用錯
> ❌ The SPCA is a charity **who** helps animals.  
> ✅ The SPCA is a charity **which** helps animals.  
> 💡 **charity是物，用which；people是人，用who**

### ⚠️ 錯誤3：句子結構不完整
> ❌ We can support a food charity prepares food for people in need.  
> ✅ We can support a food charity **which** prepares food for people in need.  
> 💡 **需要關係代詞 who/which 來連接兩個句子**

## 五、練習建議

### 短期（1週）
1. **熟記句型結構**：原因 + , + so + 結果
2. **區分 who/which**：人用who，物用which
3. **造句練習**：用每個 grammar 造3個句子

### 中期（2-4週）
1. **組合句子專練**：給兩句，練習用 so/who/which 組合
2. **改錯練習**：找出並修正常見錯誤
3. **角色扮演**：模仿課文對話，練習口說

### 長期（1個月+）
1. **寫作應用**：在作文中使用 so 和 who/which
2. **閱讀理解**：在課文中識別這些语法結構
3. **綜合運用**：結合其他 grammar 做複習
