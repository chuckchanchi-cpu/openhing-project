# 📝 English Composition — Story Writing Checklist (2026-09-24)

📋 Date: 24 September 2026 | 📖 Source: Textbook P.6 (Story Writing unit)

## E. Read the following checklist to help you write. Tick and bear each point in mind.

- [ ] **D. Put your ideas into paragraphs.**
- [ ] **Write in three paragraphs:**
  - **Paragraph 1 (Opening)** — Picture(s)
  - **Paragraph 2 (Body)** — Picture(s)
  - **Paragraph 3 (Ending)** — Picture(s)
- [ ] **The first paragraph must include:**
  - **The characters in the story** (who is in the story)
  - **The setting of the story**: time and place
- [ ] **Use linking words to connect your ideas** when you write (e.g. First, Then, Next, Finally…)
- [ ] Tick each point after you finish writing

## 💡 Key concepts (exam focus)

1. **3-paragraph structure** — every story must have:
   - Opening (Paragraph 1): introduce the **characters** and the **setting** (time + place)
   - Body (Paragraph 2): the main events of the story, matching the pictures
   - Ending (Paragraph 3): finish the story clearly
2. **Characters** — name who the story is about
3. **Setting** — always state **when** (time) and **where** (place) the story happens
4. **Connectives / linking words** — use First, Then, Next, Finally to make the story flow

## 🛠️ Techniques

- Plan first: decide characters, setting (time/place), then the 3 events for Body
- Write Opening = characters + setting in 1-2 sentences
- Use one paragraph per picture/event in the Body
- End with a clear Ending paragraph (how the story finishes / how the characters feel)
- Check every checklist point after writing (tick each box)

## ⚠️ Common traps

- ❌ Forgetting the **setting** (no time / no place) in Paragraph 1
- ❌ Mixing all ideas in **one long paragraph** instead of 3 paragraphs
- ❌ No **linking words** — sentences jump from one idea to another
- ❌ Ending paragraph missing — story stops suddenly
- ❌ Characters not introduced in the Opening (reader doesn't know who the story is about)
