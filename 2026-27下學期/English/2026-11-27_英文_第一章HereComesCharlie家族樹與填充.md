## 一、Draw Charlie's family tree. Correct the names.（亂序名字改正）
名字係**倒轉字母**（易位構詞）— 正確名如下：
| 題面亂字 | 正確名 | 身份 |

| Grandpa oJe | **Joe** | Charlie 嫲嫲/爺爺輩 |
| Grandma oJinehsp | **Josephine** | 嫲嫲 |

家族結構：Joe＋Josephine（兩老）→ Mr Bucket；George＋Georgina（兩老）→ Mrs Bucket；Mr＋Mrs Bucket → Charlie。四個祖父母＋爸媽＋Charlie ＝ **7 人**。

## 二、Cloze Passage: Charlie's Family（詞庫填充）
Word box：bed、seven、soup、floor、toothpaste、chocolate

1. There are **seven** people in the house.（Charlie＋mom＋dad＋four grandparents）
2. The house only has two rooms and one **bed**.
3. Charlie, his mom, and his dad sleep on the **floor** in the other room.
4. He puts caps on tubes at a **toothpaste** factory.
5. they eat cabbage **soup** every night for dinner.
6. they buy him one bar of **chocolate**. It is his favourite treat.

（學生手寫：seven/bed/floor/toothpaste/soup/chocolate — 全部啱 ✅）

## 📖 生字與句式
- **family tree** 家譜｜**grandparents** 祖父母｜**wooden house** 木屋
- **Even though…** 儘管……（讓步句）｜**favourite treat** 最愛的美食
- 倒轉字母認字技巧（oJe→Joe、teckBu→Bucket）— 考觀察力

## ⚠️ 考點陷阱
1. **數人頭**：four grandparents＋mom＋dad＋Charlie ＝ seven（唔係 six）
2. one **bed** 唔係 one room（原文 two rooms and one bed）
3. **toothpaste** factory（牙膏廠）— 易寫錯 toothpest
4. cabbage **soup**（忌廉湯）— 唔係 cabbage soup 之外嘅詞
5. **Even though** 跟主句（不可用 but 重複轉折）
