## 課文結構
- **A.** 瀏覽 Hong Kong Disney Resort 網站認識規則（hotel-rules-regulations 頁）
- **B.** Complete the rules with suitable words（填充 7 條酒店規則）

## B 部填充答案（官方規則用詞）
1. Each room can accommodate up to a maximum of **4** guests. At least one adult must be a staying guest in each room.
2. **Pets** (with the exception of service animals) may not be brought into the hotel.
3. **Ball** playing, frisbee throwing or other sporting activities are not allowed.
4. **Bird** feeding is prohibited.
5. Playing with **drones** or remote-controlled flying devices is not allowed.
6. Riding **bicycles**, tricycles, **scooters** or other vehicles with wheels are not permitted.
7. **Safety** comes first. Engaging in any unsafe act is not allowed.

（學生手寫：4 / Pets / Ball / Bird / drones / bicycles, scooters / Safety — 全部對 ✅）

## 📖 生字與句式（考試重點）
- **accommodate** 容納｜**maximum** 最多｜**prohibited** 禁止｜**permitted** 允許
- **with the exception of** 除……之外｜**engage in** 從事/進行｜**staying guest** 住客
- 句式：**may not be brought / are not allowed / are not permitted**（被動式規則句）
- **Safety comes first.** 安全第一（常用句子）

## ⚠️ 考點陷阱
1. **maximum of 4 guests** — 數字題，唔好寫 6
2. **Pets** 唔可以寫 animals（原詞係 Pets，有括號例外說明）
3. **Bird feeding**（餵鳥）唔係 animal feeding（照原文）
4. vehicles with **wheels** 系列：bicycles / tricycles / **scooters**（學生易漏第二空）
5. 被動式三連：may not be brought / are not allowed / are not permitted（唔好混寫）
